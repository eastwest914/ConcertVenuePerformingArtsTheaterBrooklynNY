Our Story

A Century of Culture in Brooklyn

Oceana Theater is an ideal venue for concerts, theatrical performances, comedy shows, as well as corporate events, conferences, political meetings, and so on. Our stage has welcomed artists, performers and audiences from across New York City and the world.

Oceana Theater was opened in 1934 and has been a cornerstone of Brooklyn's cultural life ever since. Known later on as the Atlantic Oceana, the Millennium Theater and the Master Theater, in 2022 the theater returned to its historical name — a proud recognition of its deep roots in the community.

Today it is a modern, excellently equipped 1326-seat venue located in the heart of South Brooklyn's immensely popular Brighton Beach area, by the beautiful ocean beaches, near convenient transportation means including the subway and easy driving access.

1934 — Grand Opening

Oceana Theater opened its doors in Brighton Beach, Brooklyn, becoming an instant cultural landmark for the community.

Mid-Century — Atlantic Oceana Era

Known as Atlantic Oceana, the venue hosted decades of celebrated performances, concerts and community events.

Later Years — Millennium & Master Theater

Operated as the Millennium Theater and later the Master Theater, continuing its tradition of world-class entertainment.

2022 — Return to Oceana Theater

The theater proudly returned to its original historic name, fully modernized with 1326 seats and state-of-the-art facilities.

When was Oceana Theater founded?
